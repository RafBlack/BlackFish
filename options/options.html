      03

      OPTIONAL / EXPERIMENTAL

      A second opinion.

      AI can offer advice in the popup. It cannot change the local score, block a submission, or authorize one. Its additional detection value has not been established.

      WHAT LEAVES YOUR BROWSER

      Only the hostname, matched rule IDs, and whether credential fields exist. No raw titles, full URLs, page text, HTML, field values, or credentials. Hostnames may still reveal a site you visit to your selected provider.

      Keys are restricted to extension pages and its worker, not encrypted in a secrets vault. Enabling AI requests network access to the supported endpoint. Limit: eight requests per document, thirty per hour, with a three-second deadline.

      04

      OPTIONAL / INVITED BETA

      Xelu's shared memory.

      Reviewed threats can warn as a page opens. Local scores and submission decisions stay independent. All cloud features start off; each needs its own consent.

      YOUR CHOICE TO SHARE

      Lookups send a hostname and the hash of the exact address, including its query. Hashing is not anonymity. Managed AI receives the hostname, matched rule IDs and credential presence; reviewed examples may be included. No page text or field values are sent. Connection metadata is visible to Cloudflare. Trusted sites, private addresses, intranet names and incognito pages are excluded.

      Only the AI provider you enable runs. Managed AI is advisory, quota-limited and never automatically falls back to your personal provider. Explicit reports permit XeluLabs human review and approved feature reuse; raw reports expire after 30 days. See the privacy documentation.
