Settings

Your privacy. Your call.

Local detection runs offline. Cloud lookups, managed AI and reports each start off and need separate consent.

Optional lookups

Lookups send your hostname and a hash of the exact address, including its query. Hashing is not anonymity. Your invited installation and connection metadata remain visible to the service and Cloudflare.

Optional AI

Managed AI receives only hostname, rule IDs and credential presence, plus reviewed examples. No raw addresses, titles, page content, screenshots or field values are sent to the model. AI remains advisory and starts behind an evaluation gate.

Explicit reports

A report sends the exact displayed address and detection metadata only after preview and confirmation of human review and minimized-feature reuse. Query strings can contain private data. Cancel if the address includes private identifiers or tokens. Reports remain pending until independent review.

Controls and retention

Disable each cloud feature in Settings. Trusted origins, incognito pages, localhost, private IP literals and intranet names are excluded from automatic requests. Use trust for public hostnames pointing to private services. Clear local caches separately from settings and credentials.

Local caches are capped at 3 MiB or 5,000 entries. Threat reviews expire within 24 hours; local copies require refresh within 15 minutes. AI advice expires after 24 hours. Raw reports are deleted after 30 days; approved minimized examples remain until revoked. Clearing a local cache does not delete submitted reports.

Beta administration

Contact the XeluLabs administrator who issued your invitation to request revocation of an installation, report or example. No public privacy contact or public release is configured yet. Application logs exclude request bodies, raw report addresses, credentials and prompts; infrastructure providers may retain operational metadata. Data is used only to provide and improve phishing protection.
